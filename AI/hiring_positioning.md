# Hiring Positioning

## Current Title
Technical Operations Manager at SuperPlay (Dice Dreams)

## Target Roles
1. **Product Owner** — mobile gaming studios
2. **Game Product Manager** — feature ownership, LiveOps
3. **LiveOps Producer/PM** — event design, feature delivery, KPI follow-up

## Key Differentiators

### 1. Hands-on prototyping (rare for PO candidates)
Most PO candidates write specs. Alon builds playable prototypes to validate ideas before pitching them. Lucky Tower, Merge Lab, Beat Runner, Draft21 all demonstrate this.

### 2. Live-game delivery experience
Current role at SuperPlay involves coordinating real feature delivery across Product, Monetization, QA, and R&D on a live game with real players. This isn't academic.

### 3. Structured product thinking
Every case study shows: define the problem → design the system → plan metrics → iterate. This is PO methodology applied consistently.

### 4. Honest positioning
"I don't call myself a Product Owner by title" builds trust. Not overselling.

## What to Emphasize (priority order for recruiter scanning)

1. **SuperPlay / Dice Dreams** — current role, real studio, live game. This should be the FIRST thing visible.
2. **Lucky Tower** — strongest product thinking showcase: KPI plan, success criteria, iteration story
3. **Merge Lab** — shows systems thinking about tooling and balancing workflows
4. **Draft21** — shipped product with real users, end-to-end execution
