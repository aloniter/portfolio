# Content Rules

## No Generic Filler
- Every sentence must say something specific
- If you can copy-paste it into any portfolio and it still works, it's too generic
- "Passionate about creating innovative solutions" — delete
- "Designed a weekly risk-reward event with reward logic and KPI planning" — keep

## No Fake-Sounding Buzzwords
Banned words: leveraged, synergy, innovative, cutting-edge, world-class, passionate, driven, dynamic, robust, seamless, best-in-class, disrupted, empowered

## No Empty Claims Without Evidence
- "Dramatically improved retention" — what were the numbers?
- "Led a major initiative" — what was it? Who was involved?
- If you can't back it up, don't say it. Honest is better than inflated.

## Every Project Must Communicate
1. **What it is** — in one sentence
2. **Why it matters** — the problem or opportunity
3. **What I did** — specific actions and decisions
4. **Why it's credible** — evidence, metrics, live demo, or real usage

## Copy Structure
- Headlines: 5-10 words, clear about what follows
- Subheads: context or type label, sentence case
- Descriptions: 1-2 sentences, action-verb start, concrete detail
- Paragraphs: 2-3 sentences max
- Lists: action verbs first, parallel structure, 3-7 items

## Tone
- Direct but not aggressive
- Confident but not arrogant
- Honest about limitations
- Human, not corporate
- Professional, not casual
- Specific, not vague

## For Recruiters
- Card descriptions should be understandable without clicking through
- Role titles and project types should use industry-standard terms
- "Product Owner fit" messaging should be consistent across pages
- Contact info and CV download should be easy to find
