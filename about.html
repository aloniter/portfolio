About

I sit between product and building, and I'd rather be in both.

I run LiveOps on Dice Dreams at SuperPlay — a live game with millions of players — and I build my own products alongside it: a Unity game, an iOS app that's on the App Store, and the internal automation that takes repeated work off the team.

My title says operations and I'm not going to dress that up. The part I can't switch off is building: when something's slow I write the tool, and when I have an idea for a game I build it rather than write it down. What I want next is to own more of what gets built.
