BlockQuest

Unity mobile puzzle battler prototype where block-placement strategy drives boss-fight progression.

Role Solo Game Designer & Prototype Developer
Status Active prototype development
Platform Unity / Portrait Mobile
Focus Puzzle loop, boss pressure, and UX clarity

BlockQuest is currently in active prototype development. There is no public demo link yet, so this case study focuses on the current game loop, systems direction, and product thinking behind the prototype.

Screens

Product Goal

Create a compact portrait mobile game that combines the familiar satisfaction of block-placement puzzles with the tension of boss fights, enemy attacks, and escalating encounters.

My Role

  • Designed the core loop from piece placement to line clears, boss damage, enemy attacks, and progression
  • Built the Unity prototype direction around an 8x8 board, three-piece tray, HP states, and mobile-safe UI
  • Defined feedback moments for combos, damage reactions, boss pressure, rewards, and failure states
  • Scoped the prototype around retention, progression, replayability, and fast iteration

Problem / Opportunity

Block puzzles are easy to understand, but they can become flat when every clear has the same weight. The opportunity in BlockQuest is to make each clear feel like part of a fight: every row or column pushes the boss closer to defeat, and every boss attack changes the board state in a way the player has to solve around.

Core Loop

1. Place pieces

Choose from a three-piece tray and make board decisions that preserve future placement options.

2. Clear lines

Complete rows and columns to create readable damage moments and combo opportunities.

3. Damage the boss

Turn puzzle success into combat progress through boss HP, reactions, and encounter states.

4. Survive attacks

Boss attacks add pressure by disrupting the board and forcing the player to recover.

Key Systems

8x8 Puzzle Board

Keeps the rules readable while giving enough space for strategy, recovery, and combo setup.

Three-Piece Tray

Creates short-term planning pressure and makes every placement affect the next decision.

Boss HP Feedback

Converts line clears into visible combat progress so the board and boss fight feel connected.

Attack Pressure

Uses boss attacks and obstacles to create tension beyond pure high-score optimization.

Encounter Progression

Escalating bosses give the prototype a clear arcade progression path and replay goal.

Mobile-First UI

Portrait layout, safe-area thinking, and readable hierarchy keep the game usable on phones.

Design Direction

The product direction is not only to prove that the mechanics work, but to test whether the loop has the right motivational shape for a mobile game: fast understanding, repeated tension, satisfying feedback, and a reason to continue into the next encounter.

  • Use boss HP and attack timing to make puzzle clears feel consequential
  • Keep the board readable so new pressure does not turn into visual noise
  • Make combo clears feel stronger through feedback, pacing, and damage reactions
  • Scope progression around replayability, difficulty pacing, and future live-ops hooks

Success Criteria

  • Players understand the board, tray, and boss objective within the first few seconds
  • Line clears immediately communicate damage, progress, and combat impact
  • Boss attacks create pressure without making the board feel unfair or unreadable
  • Early encounters teach the loop while later fights add meaningful difficulty
  • The prototype supports quick tuning of HP, attack cadence, pieces, and encounter pacing

Next Iterations

  • Tune boss HP curves, attack cadence, and early encounter difficulty
  • Add more boss behaviors that change board pressure without adding rule confusion
  • Expand reward and relic progression into a clearer long-term motivation layer
  • Track clears, failed boards, boss defeats, and attack outcomes to guide balance decisions

Active Prototype

BlockQuest is being used to test mobile puzzle-battler mechanics, UI hierarchy, encounter pacing, and progression systems before a public build is shared.
