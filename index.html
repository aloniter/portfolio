The day job

LiveOps on a game with millions of players.

I'm a Technical Operations Manager at SuperPlay, on Dice Dreams. Live events, campaigns and A/B tests go out through me: configuration, validation, rollout, and the follow-up when a number moves the wrong way. It's why I'm not guessing at how a live game operates at scale.

Deliberately light on specifics: no confidential data, unreleased features or internal numbers.
