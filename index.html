How I Work

The same four moves, every time.

Step 01

Notice what's annoying

Something's slow, or confusing, or takes four manual steps it shouldn't. I don't wait to be assigned it. I go look at why it's like that.

Step 02

Work out how it really runs

The loop, the economy, the data model, who touches what. I want the rules underneath before I touch anything you can see.

Step 03

Build the smallest real version

Not a doc, not a deck. Something you can click through or play. If I can't get it working, I didn't understand it well enough yet.

Step 04

Get it in front of people

Ship it, watch what actually happens, fix what's wrong. A rough thing people use beats a polished idea that never left my laptop.
