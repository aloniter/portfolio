Iron Dom

Mobile defense arcade game built around fast reactions, targeting, and city protection.

Role Solo Designer & Developer
Timeline Personal portfolio prototype
Platform Mobile Web
Focus Timing, targeting, and wave pressure
Play Live Demo

Screens

Product Goal

Create a tense but accessible arcade experience where the player protects a city by identifying incoming threats, reacting quickly, and launching interceptors before missiles reach their targets.

Core Gameplay

Players control a defensive system that scans the sky for incoming missiles. The loop is simple and immediate, but each wave adds more pressure and forces faster prioritization.

  • Incoming missiles appear from different directions
  • The player detects and targets the most dangerous threats
  • Interceptors are launched to destroy missiles mid-air
  • The player survives increasingly difficult waves
  • Score is based on accuracy, reaction speed, and city protection

Design Goals

The game is built around clarity, quick reactions, and strong visual feedback so the player always understands the threat, their response, and the result.

  • Fast and readable gameplay
  • Clear player objective
  • Increasing difficulty over time
  • Satisfying interception feedback
  • Simple controls suitable for quick play sessions
  • Strong visual connection between threat, reaction, and result

Technical Side

Iron Dom demonstrates my ability to create interactive gameplay systems, structure a playable prototype, and design a clear game loop from idea to execution.

  • Game logic and state management
  • Collision detection
  • Player input handling
  • UI feedback and scoring systems
  • Difficulty progression
  • Real-time interaction
  • Browser-based game development

Why I Built It

I created Iron Dom to practice building a complete gameplay loop around a simple but powerful concept: protect the city under pressure. The project reflects my interest in games that are easy to understand, immediately playable, and built around strong player feedback.

It also demonstrates my ability to take a real-world-inspired idea and translate it into an engaging arcade mechanic suitable for a web-based portfolio game.
